Support

Something wrong, missing or confusing? Email support@usetoque.com and we'll get back to you.

A restaurant's menu, prices or hours are wrong

Tell us the restaurant and what's changed. If you can, include a link to the venue's own website or a photo of its menu or door sign. Menus show the date they were checked, so older prices may have changed.

A place has closed, or is missing

On a place's page, answer Still open? to hide a closed place from your results, or email us so we can update it for everyone.

I own a restaurant listed in Toque

We read menus and hours from your own website or ordering page. Email us from your business address to correct your listing, send an updated menu, or ask for it to be removed.

A public post is inappropriate

Open the post and choose Report. Posts reported by several people are hidden until we review them. You can also block a person so you no longer see their posts. For anything urgent, email us with the restaurant and the post's nickname.

Deleting my data

You can delete your public posts in the app. To delete everything tied to your account, see Deleting your data in the privacy policy.

Requirements

Toque runs on iPhone. Restaurant coverage is the Australian Capital Territory.
