Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Toque is an iPhone app for finding restaurants in Canberra by the dishes they serve. It is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app keeps, where, and what you can do about it. In short: most of what you do stays on your phone, we never ask for your name or email, and there are no ads and no tracking. The one thing Toque sends us about how you use it is an anonymous copy of your searches, which you can turn off.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location. If you allow it, Toque uses your location on your phone to show distances, walking times and what's open near you. It is not sent to us.
  • Weather. To show weather, Toque sends the centre of the suburb you are in (not your exact position) to Apple WeatherKit. The weather is shown on screen and is not stored by us or kept on your phone.
  • Saved places and your food diary. These are stored only on your phone. They are not synced to iCloud or sent to us.
  • "Still open?" answers. When you tell Toque a place is open or closed, the answer is kept on your phone.

Your searches

Searches are answered on your phone. No server takes part in finding results.

To help us improve search, Toque also sends us an anonymous copy of your searches. This is on by default. For each search it sends:

  • the words you searched for;
  • how many places came back;
  • which place you opened, by its position in the list, if you opened one.

Nothing that identifies you goes with them: no account, no device ID, no location and no time. Searches are sent in small batches, at most once an hour. Each batch carries only the app's version number, and our server notes only the date it arrives. A batch that fails to send is dropped, not kept for later.

Before sending, your phone leaves out searches that look like contact details, such as phone numbers, email addresses, usernames and web links. Our server checks again and drops any it finds.

We use these searches to find what returns nothing or the wrong places, and to test and improve search. They are never shown to other users, sold or shared. They are deleted from our server after 90 days. A search that many people make (seen at least 3 times, on at least 2 days) may be kept longer in our private test set, after we check it by hand for names and contact details. Only the words are kept there, with how many times and on how many days they were searched and the day they first appeared.

You can turn this off at any time in the app: Me → Privacy → Help improve search. Searches that have not been sent yet are deleted from your phone when you turn it off.

What you share when you post publicly

Browsing public posts needs no account. A post stays private, on your phone only, unless you choose to make it public. To make a post public, comment, like, report, block, send a contribution or add friends, Toque asks you to sign in with your Apple Account.

Sign in with Apple gives our server a stable user ID that Apple creates for Toque. The app does not ask Apple for your name or email address, and our server would not keep them if they were sent. If you sign in, our server also keeps an encrypted token from Apple, used only to remove Toque from your Sign in with Apple list when you delete your account.

Public posts and everything that goes with them are stored on Toque's own server, which runs on Cloudflare (Workers, a D1 database and R2 file storage). A public post can be seen by anyone using the app. We store:

  • the post: its photo pages, text, the optional longer text (up to 500 characters), rating, dish names and the restaurant it is about, with the nickname you chose (never your real name);
  • your comments, which show your nickname to everyone;
  • your likes, which others see only as a count;
  • your reports and blocks;
  • contributions you send for a place, such as opening hours or photos of a menu, and a new place you add (its name, address and map pin). Menu photos can be seen only by you and the developer until the developer approves the menu; an approved menu's photos are then shown on the place's page;
  • the Sign in with Apple user ID above, so the app knows which posts are yours.

Photos are uploaded as you see them in the post.

Menu reading. We use the menu photos you send, with the text your phone read from them and the dishes the developer approves, to run, test and train the model that reads menus for Toque, privately on our server and on Kaggle, a machine-learning service; rejected menus are not used for training.

Checks on what you post. Before a post becomes public, Toque checks its photos for nudity on your phone, using Apple's Sensitive Content Analysis (where your phone has it turned on) and a model bundled in the app. Toque also looks in the photos, on your phone, for QR codes, barcodes and contact details such as phone numbers, links, email addresses or usernames, and a photo with one can't be posted. The photos are not sent anywhere for these checks. Public text (nicknames, notes, the longer text of a post, dish names and comments) is checked against a word list on our server before it is accepted.

Reports. Reports are used to hide posts and comments that several people report, and are reviewed by the developer. Reports are anonymous in the app: the person you report and other users never see who reported. Your report is stored with your Toque user ID, which only the developer can look up in the database. Blocks are visible only to you, and the person you block is not told.

Friends

You can add people you know as friends. A friendship needs both people: one asks and the other accepts. There is no following.

  • Over 18 only. Before you can add friends, Toque asks Apple, through Declared Age Range, whether you are 18 or over. If you are, our server keeps only the time you confirmed. It never receives your birthday or age. If you are not, or you don't answer, nothing is sent and the answer stays on your phone.
  • Finding people. People can find you by the start of your nickname, unless you turn this off in the app. Results show your nickname, the month you joined and how many reviews you have. You can also share an invite link: our server keeps only a one-way hash of its code, and the link stops working after 7 days or 20 uses, or when you revoke it. Toque never uploads your contacts.
  • Friends and requests. We store who is friends with whom, and requests until they are answered or expire after 30 days. "Not now" is silent: the person who asked is not told. Removing a friend is quiet too. Blocking someone ends the friendship and hides each of you from the other.
  • Posts for friends. When you post, you choose Everyone or Friends. A friends-only post is shown only to your friends, while neither of you has blocked the other.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, your phone gives Toque a device token, which our server keeps with your account to tell you when someone asks to be your friend, when someone accepts and when you get a chat message (see Chat). They are delivered through Apple's Push Notification service and show the other person's nickname only, and for a place that was shared, its name. The token is deleted when you sign out or delete your account.

Chat

You can chat with your friends, one to one or in a group. Chat is not end-to-end encrypted: our server can read what you send, as described below.

  • Who can chat. Only friends, and only people who are 18 or over (see Friends). A one-to-one chat needs a friendship. Nobody can join a group by link: members are added by a member who is their friend.
  • What we store. We store your messages, the places you share in chat, and votes (a question, its options and who chose what). We also store each chat's members, the group's title, when each message was sent, how far you have read, and when you muted a chat. The text of messages, shared places and votes is encrypted on our server with a key that we hold, and is decrypted when a member opens the chat. The group title, who sent what and when are not encrypted.
  • Editing. You can edit a text message for 15 minutes after you send it, up to 5 times. Chat members see the new text marked as edited. We keep the earlier versions, encrypted like the message, for as long as the message exists. Other members cannot see them. They are used only if the message is reported, when up to the last 5 earlier versions go into the report.
  • Unsend. You can delete your own message at any time. Its text is removed from our server, along with any earlier versions, and the chat shows "Message deleted". Deleting a message does not remove a report that was already filed about it (see Reports below).
  • Word filter. Text you send in chat is checked against a word list on our server, and slurs and sexual words are refused. Group titles are also checked for insults. Chat does not block links or contact details.
  • Reports and blocks. You can report a message, a group or a person, and block a person. A report sends the developer a copy of the reported message as it was when you reported it, with its earlier versions if it was edited, or the group's title and size, or the person's nickname. The copy is stored in readable form and with your Toque user ID. The developer's moderation tools show these copies only, not the other messages in the chat. A message is hidden after 3 people report it. A report can also pause sending for the person reported until the developer has looked. Blocking someone stops one-to-one messages between you and hides their messages from you in groups.
  • How long we keep it. Messages are deleted after one year, and a chat keeps at most its last 10,000 messages. Chat reports, with their copies, are deleted one year after they are filed. A clean-up job removes old messages every 15 minutes, and old reports once a day.
  • Leaving. If you leave a group, you stop receiving it. If you delete a chat, its history is hidden from you only, and it comes back with the next message. Your messages stay for the other members. A group is deleted when its last member leaves.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, a new chat message sends a push through Apple's Push Notification service. It shows the group's title or the sender's nickname, and a line such as "sent a message", "shared" a place by name, "started a vote" or "added you". It never includes the text of a message. A burst of messages rings once, and muted chats send none.

Bans

The developer can ban an account that breaks the community rules. A banned account's posts and comments are hidden, and it is signed out. So that it cannot simply sign up again, we keep a one-way hash of its Sign in with Apple user ID, which cannot be turned back into the ID. This hash stays after the account is deleted and is used only to refuse sign-in.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and no selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, and no location history.

Services Toque relies on

  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, Apple Maps (maps, directions and finding a place when you post) and WeatherKit (weather). Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts Toque's server, database and photo storage. Cloudflare sees the network address of each request. We use it only to limit abuse, and it is not saved in our database. Cloudflare's operational logs keep request details for about 3 days. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account in the app: Me → Delete my account (the last row on the Me page, shown while you are signed in). This deletes your nickname, public posts and their photos, your comments, likes, reports, blocks and contributions (with their menu photos), and signs you out. It also deletes your friends, the friend requests you sent and received, your invite links and your notification device tokens. From Chat, it deletes your messages and the places you shared, which disappear for the other members too, your answers in votes, your mutes and read positions, the chat reports you filed and the chat reports about you. Group chats stay for the other members. Likes, comments and reports that other people left on your posts go with the posts. A place you added that the developer has already approved stays as a shared place, without your name. If your account was banned, the one-way hash described under Bans stays.
  • The database rows are deleted at once. Photo files are removed from storage within moments, and an automatic clean-up that runs every 15 minutes retries any that fail. Cloudflare keeps short-lived point-in-time backups of the database (7 days on our current plan, at most 30). Deleted data can remain in those backups until they expire, and is not restored. Toque also asks Apple to remove Toque from your Sign in with Apple list.
  • You can delete a single public post in the app at any time; it is removed from the public feed and its photos are deleted, and it stays in your diary on your phone.
  • Data that stays on your phone is deleted when you delete it in the app or remove the app.
  • Anonymous copies of searches are not tied to you, so we cannot find or delete yours. They are deleted after 90 days, as described under Your searches.
  • If you can no longer use the app, email support@usetoque.com and we will delete everything tied to your account. We will confirm within 30 days.
  • You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for Toque in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

Children

Toque is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
